## Summary

1. Constant game: the sandwich tower-defense. Totals: podium 4-4 (losses: racer clear x2, racer slight, terrace slight), floor 5-0, concept 0-6 (all decisive).

   **Change first: the camera and the scale of the gameplay pieces.** Drop the camera to a low 3/4 angle with perspective, a visible back wall and horizon, and depth of field, so the olives and towers read at 3 to 4 times their current size. Every concept frame and both racer wins come from a camera inside the world with a hero object large in frame. The game's top-down, far camera makes the actors (olives about 2% of frame height, toast crossbows) disappear, so the set dressing has to carry the look. That would narrow all 6 concept pairs and plausibly flip the 2 slight podium losses.

   **Next two:**
   - Towers and enemies as hero characters: stacked club-sandwich towers on cork coasters and big-eyed olives, as in the concept. The current toast-slice-plus-crossbow reads as a flat placeholder from above.
   - Replace the airbrushed spray-paint path (orange, blue, pink haze) with an authored surface: a groove, toothpick rail or fabric runner. The gingham level's stitched runner is already the right idea. Also give the grey levels (stovetop, marble) the same warm key light and grade as the wood and bar levels. Those are the weakest game frames.

2. Bugs and placeholders seen:
   - Sandwich-on-plate goal is tipped or billboarded toward camera in landscape (concept 01-06, left side), so the plate reads as standing on edge.
   - Landscape bottom HUD is clipped: "12 olives", "8 green" and similar subtitles are cut off at the bottom edge (all concept pairs).
   - Floating text collides with the wave banner ("Brine time!" under "WAVE 3", podium 05).
   - Faint ghost text "Too quick!" left at the top of the path (podium 01).
   - Concept 06 subtitle says "8 green" where all other levels say "olives", which looks like a placeholder label.
   - Bottom-row props are cut by the frame edge (half plates, glass at right edge in gingham), which reads as uncomposed rather than as a bug.
